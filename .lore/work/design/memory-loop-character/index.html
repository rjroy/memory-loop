One family, not one mood. These reduced UI studies explore the existing Guide identity through four different emotional jobs.

Every note, conversation and goal below is illustrative. Samples are static drawings: nothing opens, saves or sends.

Ground Inspiring Capture A blank slate Think Curious Recall Reflective

Ground

Inspiring

An invitation, not a dashboard report. Full, unaltered A.1 artwork brings the world into the place where you reconnect with your intentions. An expressive welcome sits alongside—not over—the image. Familiar threads and the longer view share the space.

Capture

A blank slate

Quiet by choice, not without character. A Parchment writing surface, the familiar wordmark and one Lantern action. No illustration, quote, prompt collection or decorative interruption: put the thought down and go.

Think

Curious

Leave the question open. The cool side of the palette makes room for possibility; a warm question keeps it human. The small A.5 signature belongs at the invitation, not behind a working conversation. Typography carries the curiosity.

Recall

Reflective

Recognition before decoration. The same dark navigation frames a warm, editorial reading surface. Libre Baskerville gives the returning thought its own voice. Imagery stays in the margin; no landscape sits behind the words.

What makes them family

One recognizable signature. The original Guide mark, an IM Fell wordmark and the same four destinations. Not a new logo or a new fictional world.

Three voices, with different jobs. IM Fell English SC for identity and invitations; Source Sans 3 for working UI; Libre Baskerville for editorial reflection.

Light and color have a purpose. Night holds the frame; Parchment opens up writing and reading. Lantern invites action; Horizon marks selections against Night. These are board proposals, not changes to the live app or a decision on its focus colors.

The thing to judge: do these feel like four rooms in a place you’d want to return to?
