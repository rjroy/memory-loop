# Direction review and approval record

Companion to [the shell shape](phase-1-shell.md), not a second product authority. The original shell passed acceptance under the [plan](../plans/phase-1-shell.md). After trying its phone/Tailscale preview, the user requested dark styling: the same personal-brand system and layout, with a dark default rather than a theme switch. That CSS-only refinement is now built/visually checked and awaits parent-owned independent verification/review; original GREEN/Accept/ship evidence is historical, not reused as its fresh verdict. No standing build-path preference changes.

## Current approved direction: personal-brand shell

Use **Night `#031315`** for workspace and navigation; **Parchment `#FEF6E1`** for readable text, thin notice boundaries, and focus outlines; **Lantern `#EFB83F`** for the restrained selection marker and existing selection/skip-link highlight with Night text; **Horizon `#5E8BD6`** remains reserved and unused. Keep **Source Sans 3 / Source Sans 3 Semibold** throughout the interface. No new hue, art, typography, toggles, or behavior is added. The [local guidance snapshot](personal-brand-snapshot.md) records source revisions/status and the unchanged two-font/OFL copy manifest.

**First viewport and path:** the built dark shell retains the same four left-rail destinations on desktop, four equal bottom links on phone, route title/purpose, and honest unavailable-feature region. **Signature:** the Lantern edge and semibold label show selection without moving the shell. **Focus:** Parchment outlines remain separate from selection on the Night field; content links are Parchment and underlined. Before/after desktop/phone geometry and copy are identical. The shell brief records actual checks and pending independent acceptance at their proper scope. **Reach:** future view replacements remain independent. **Risk:** neither dark mode nor personal branding justifies glow, imagery, new controls, or promotion; none are added.

The former mineral-green/Public Sans appearance is superseded. The user's pinned brand choice wins over the seed's unpinned palette/type suggestions; it does not require rerolling the structural concept. The subsequent authorization now permits implementation of this bounded shell, with local font integration/license provenance as planned. It does not authorize generated artwork, later domain features, settled deployment architecture, or a claim of verified accessibility.

## Historical grounding and selection record

**Mechanism:** quick capture independent of dashboard configuration, alongside AI discussion and local vault access; each destination can evolve without replacing the shell. **Scene:** one person's home desktop and phone. **Cultural grounding hypothesis:** writing tools, file/editor interfaces, and everyday wayfinding—not a researched persona or a claim about personal tastes. The first surface must prove location, reachability, and honest lack of functionality.

Avoid both category ruts: a productivity dashboard full of invented cards/activity, and its predictable opposite, a giant empty document framed as a writing retreat. The name's literal circular/loop motif is not a navigation system and is not a reason for ring graphics.

The following systems and alternatives record the **original, pre-brand exploration**. Their color/type descriptions are historical and superseded by the current approved direction above; none is active implementation guidance. Seven systems were ordered before the roll; the first was the designer's pick, not an approved choice:

## Historical lead before brand approval: studio console

**World:** flat cool-gray work surface and a deep mineral-green rail; Public Sans and crisp, low-radius link rows. Selected location alone gets the prominent edge/field treatment. **First viewport:** app name and scope above four links at left; route title, purpose, and small static availability region at right. On phone, the four destinations move intact to the bottom. **Visitor path:** Ground opens by default; select/bookmark Capture directly; Think and Recall are equally reachable. **Signature:** selected position changes immediately while the rest of the shell stays still. **Reach:** panels, composer, conversation, and file browser can each replace the outlet independently. **Risk:** literal audio styling would mislead; the shell keeps console organization, never console instruments.

The five named raises in the shell brief are part of this proposal. They add hierarchy, stable order, decisive selection, equal discoverability, and bounded text; they do not add borrowed symbols or machinery.

## Historical IMPECCABLE’S PICK: writing-desk utility tray

**World:** pale neutral surfaces, blue-black ink, restrained blue selection; Public Sans; thin dividers and gently rounded controls. **First viewport:** compact app header, four full-text links in one horizontal band, route title and availability immediately below; a wider continuous work surface rather than left-rail framing. **Visitor path:** the same four routes and truthful placeholders; phone still uses bottom navigation. **Signature:** active tab's lower rule becomes the work-surface boundary without moving the text. **Reach:** future tasks occupy a shared clear desktop sheet without inheriting a dashboard grid. **Risk:** this familiar notes-tool arrangement is effective but less distinctive; it is the comfortable category-adjacent choice. No imitation paper, serif hero, or desk illustration.

## Historical full alternate: plate-section index — competitive

**World:** the design-annual catalog's near-white `#F5F4F6`, charcoal `#16181A`, pale periwinkle `#DCDDF3`, hairline boundaries, and one grotesque. **First viewport:** four plain navigation tokens in a compact top strip; title/purpose left and availability right on desktop, stacked on phone. Status content is organized as a small editorial plate, not a chat/editor mockup. **Visitor path:** ordinary route links; selected token gets dark ink and an explicit rule; mobile becomes four bottom links. **Signature:** crisp, near-immediate inversion for selection, no spinning stamp. **Reach:** its grid can structure real future panels and document tools. **Risk:** display scale, tiny tracked labels, registration marks, and animation in the reference must yield to the task-focused brief; without discipline it becomes a design portfolio, not a personal tool.

**Comparison, exactly two axes:** audience identification is weaker than the lead's working-tool familiarity; product clarity is competitive because a strict title/purpose/state grid can expose the shell's truth equally well. **Verdict: competitive**, not a win. It remains adoptable and contributes no mixed styling to the lead.

## Historical review exits — superseded by brand approval

The original review offered the lead, tray, plate alternative, one correction, or a fresh **plain**, **safer**, or **bolder** direction round with optional steering. The user has since chosen the personal-brand adaptation rather than reopening that exploration. These exits remain a record of the process, not a pending selection request.

A quiet standing alternative is the familiar category-standard app shell, played straight. If that is preferred, identify two or three adjacent products before its implementation direction is committed. It is an available exit, not the recommendation or a standing product preference.

This remains the design decision record. `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` map the current dark-default CSS; its refinement gate is open for parent-owned independent verification/review, while the original phase's acceptance remains historical. No standing build path is stored, and no image generation or paid rendering was performed.
